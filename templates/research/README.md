# Research Templates

This directory contains reusable research artifacts for exploration and evidence gathering.

## Recommended files

```text
templates/research/
├── research-brief-template.md
├── comparison-template.md
├── findings-template.md
├── reference-game-analysis-template.md
└── domain-study-template.md
```

## Rule

Research findings are evidence, not requirements. They should inform design proposals and accepted decisions rather than silently changing scope.
