# Coordination / Handoff Skill

**Status:** Active  
**Version:** 0.1

Use this skill when work is transferred from one role to another without losing the task's objective, rationale, or constraints.

## Purpose

A good handoff preserves:

- objective;
- rationale;
- accepted decisions;
- constraints;
- assumptions;
- unresolved questions;
- progress;
- expected output;
- authority boundaries.

## Standard handoff checklist

```text
from:
  - role
  - reason for handoff

to:
  - target role
  - why this role fits

objective:
  - what must be achieved

context:
  - relevant accepted decisions
  - relevant known constraints
  - unresolved blockers

progress:
  - completed
  - in progress
  - remaining

expected output:
  - deliverable
  - acceptance criteria

authority:
  - may modify
  - may not modify
  - requires approval
```

## Rule

Never silently reinterpret accepted decisions during a handoff.
